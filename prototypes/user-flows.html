Checklist

What the prototype already does

UX

  • Entry point carries over: the date or passage you clicked is already selected in the booking.
  • Price always visible: total in the summary on every step, deposit and balance spelled out, no card fees.
  • No account: guest checkout; Telegram is optional.
  • Forms that help: autocomplete, right keyboards on phones, inline errors on blur, an error summary that takes focus.
  • Progress and going back: named steps, Back keeps what you typed (session storage).
  • Phones: collapsible summary at the top, sticky Continue button, square photos in card rows.
  • Trust at the decision point: cancellation terms and “experience checked before payment” next to the pay button.
  • No dead ends: full dates offer a waitlist; the confirmation ends with three useful actions.
  • Accessible: real labels and fieldsets, aria-current on the step, focus moves to each new step heading.

SEO

  • Titles and descriptions written per page, with place, year and starting price.
  • Four languages, four URLs: English, Russian, German and Spanish pages, each with its own address, html lang, canonical, hreflang to all four plus x-default, og:locale and translated structured data. A switcher in the header keeps you on the same page.
  • Structured data: Course with a CourseInstance and Offer for every date; BreadcrumbList; FAQPage; Organization and WebSite.
  • Visible breadcrumbs that match the BreadcrumbList data.
  • Booking pages kept out of search with noindex, follow.
  • Social previews: Open Graph and a large Twitter card.
  • Stable layout: fixed image sizes in card rows and galleries, lazy-loaded photos below the fold.

Next to build

UX

  • Live availability: hold the place for 30 minutes during payment, release it after.
  • Saved booking link: email the half-finished booking (only with consent).
  • Currency: show prices in the visitor’s currency, charge in euros.
  • Group bookings: invite crew by link so each pays their own share.

SEO

  • One page per course and per passage, so each can rank for its own search (today only Bareboat Skipper has one).
  • Server-rendered HTML for all content, and a sitemap.xml with hreflang alternates.
  • Speed: self-host fonts, preload the first gallery photo, serve AVIF/WebP.
  • FAQ data is a bonus, not a goal: Google shows FAQ rich results only for a few authoritative sites now; the questions still help readers.
  • Real reviews only if collected after the course; never marked up without them.
