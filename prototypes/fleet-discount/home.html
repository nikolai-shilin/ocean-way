Questions

Before you start

Do I need a licence to charter?

To sail without a skipper, yes: charter companies ask for a recognised licence and a short sailing CV. Without one, book a boat with a skipper, or take the Bareboat Skipper course first.

I've never sailed. Where do I start?

With Introduction to Yachting: five days on a yacht with an instructor, no experience needed. Add the one-day VHF course, which all crew need.

Can I charter one of your boats?

Yes. When a yacht is not out with students, you can charter it for a week, bareboat with a licence or with one of our skippers. Ask in Telegram which weeks are free.

Is the price final?

We confirm availability and the full price with the fleet before you pay, including deposit, cleaning and extras.
