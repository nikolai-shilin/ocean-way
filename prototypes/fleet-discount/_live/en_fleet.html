What you pay at the base

Service pack

€300–€400

Once per booking: transit log for Türkiye, final cleaning, bed linen and towels. €150 more if you sail to Greece. On catamarans the dinghy outboard is included.

Security deposit

€2 000–€4 500

Held at check-in and returned at check-out if the boat comes back as you took her.

Check-in and out

Saturday

Board from 17:00, return the following Saturday by 09:00. A skipper costs €200 a day plus food, a cook €150 a day.
