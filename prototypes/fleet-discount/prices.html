What it costs

All prices are in euros. Courses and passages are priced per person, with the boat, instructor and marina fees included; charter is the price of the whole boat for a week. For courses and passages you pay a 30% deposit to book and the rest six weeks before you sail.

Cabins and extras

What the price includes

CabinPrice
Shared double cabinWith another student of the same genderIncluded
Double cabin for twoFor couples and friends booking togetherIncluded

Included

  • Instructor for the whole week, max 4 students
  • Yacht, fuel and marina fees on the course route
  • Course book, logbook and assessment
  • Certificate fee (IYT or RYA)
  • Bed linen, towels and final cleaning

Not included

  • Food kitty, about €150 per person
  • VHF radio (SRC) course, €190, optional
  • Flights and transfer to the marina
  • Travel insurance
Payment

Payment and cancellation

How you pay

  • 30% deposit to book, the rest 6 weeks before. No card fees.
  • Card or bank transfer. You can also pay the full price at once.

Cancellation

  • Free cancellation until 6 weeks before the start. After that the deposit is kept, or you can move it to another date in the same year once.
