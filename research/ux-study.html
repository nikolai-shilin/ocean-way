What the best sailing school sites do well, and a design for Ocean Way

We read 18 sailing school sites page by page across four markets: US, UK and Europe, liveaboard schools, and Russian-language schools. This page sums up what works, what nobody does yet, and a site design built on those gaps.

Key findings

Most sailing school sites are brochures. The best few work like booking engines.

Live dates on the homepage sell courses

Sailing Virgins puts real departures on the homepage and every destination page, with spots left and a deposit to hold a place. Most schools hide dates behind a form or a "call for pricing" button.

Nobody shows the learning path well

Of 18 sites, only Glénans explains levels as skills plus recommended weeks of practice. Nobody links the course ladder to the calendar. RYA, ASA and Offshore use plain card lists.

Total cost is never in one place

Certificate fees, kitty, marina fees, membership and tips come out late or not at all. Moscow Sailing Academy adds 22,000 ₽ for the certificate. Glénans adds a €34 membership. No site shows an all-in total.

People and boats are missing from the sale

No site ties a date to a specific instructor and boat. Instructor bios, where they exist, sit on separate pages. Reviews are rarely tied to a course and are often years old.

The data contradicts itself everywhere

Different nights, crew sizes, miles, boat types, phone numbers and prices for the same course turned up on 13 of 18 sites. Running every page from one record per departure fixes this.

Russian-speaking buyers want Telegram, clear licence advice and instalments

Messengers appear in most headers, yet every site still ends in "leave a form, we'll call back". The question "which licence is valid where?" is the biggest worry, and only Navigator answers it with a table.

Design proposal

Ocean Way: learn to skipper on a real route, with every date, cost and person visible

Assumptions to confirm: Ocean Way is an IYT-accredited school. Theory is taught online. Practice happens in liveaboard weeks on warm-water routes like Turkey, Sardinia, Seychelles and Phuket. The site is in Russian and English. All prices and dates in the mockups are sample data.

Show the path

One interactive ladder from first sail to Yachtmaster. Each level says what you will be allowed to do, how long it takes, the price and the next date. A short quiz picks your starting level.

Book from the date

Every course card and course page lists real dates with berths left, the boat, the instructor and a Hold button with a 20% deposit. "Notify me" on full weeks, "suggest my dates" when nothing fits.

One all-in price

Course, certificate, logbook, kitty, marina fees and transfer added into one total, with the payment plan, cancellation terms and ₽/€ shown next to the button.

Real people, one account

Meet the instructor and crew before you sail. Online theory, booked weeks, sea miles and certificates live in one logbook account, which brings people back for the next level.

Visual identity

Taken from the nautical chart

Most competitors use navy-and-white sailing clichés or stock photos. Ocean Way's style comes from the chart students learn to read: chart-water blues, buff for land and missing items, and chart magenta, the colour of lights and routes, as the one accent. This page uses the same style.

Deep waterlinks, data

Archivo Expanded
Public Sans for running text. Readable at small sizes on a phone in bright sun, and it covers Cyrillic.
IBM Plex Mono · 36°37′N 29°06′E · 120 nm · 14–21 Nov

Rules: magenta only on the main action and the route line. Soundings and coordinates are used as texture. Photos come only from real courses, captioned with place and date. Archivo needs a Cyrillic check: if its Cyrillic is weak, use Unbounded or Golos Text for Russian headlines.

Data model and roadmap

One record per departure feeds every page

Contradictory facts were the most common flaw in the study. Store each fact once and generate cards, course pages, the calendar, the booking widget and JSON-LD from it. This matches the Supabase setup already used in the RYC site.

level

  • order, name
  • can_do (text)
  • prereq_miles, prereq_days
  • next_level

course

  • level_id, format
  • days, theory_hours
  • included[], excluded[]
  • day_plan[]

departure

  • course, route, dates
  • boat_id, instructor_id
  • berths_total / booked
  • price_lines[] → all-in
  • deposit_pct, cancel_rule

boat · instructor

  • model, cabins, photos
  • bio, miles, credentials
  • reviews[]

student

  • bookings, payments
  • log_entries (nm, nights)
  • certificates
